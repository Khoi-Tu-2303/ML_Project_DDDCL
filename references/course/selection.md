## Objectives

Every team starts the project by choosing the paper it will carry for the whole
semester. The choice matters more than it looks: a good pick survives a semester
of reading, rebuilding, and stress testing, and feeds every later checkpoint.
This milestone has no code and no report. Its deliverable is the registration
itself, and its real work is the deliberation that precedes it.

## The selection form

```{important}
The team registers by filling the **selection form published on Moodle**.
Moodle is the only submission channel. Read the form carefully before you
decide, because it is the full rulebook of this milestone.
```

## After the registration

Form submissions flow into the **Google Sheet** linked to the form, and the
instructor reviews the registrations there, accepting papers and marking the
acceptances in the sheet itself. From that acceptance on, the paper binds the
team for the rest of the semester, and a later change needs explicit
instructor approval. Once the paper is accepted, the work moves to
{ref}`intro-ml-m2-reading`, where the paper goes under the microscope.
